# ContextMenu

<Lede>
  ContextMenu replaces the browser's right-click menu on one region with your own actions. It opens
  at the pointer, and once open it behaves like a [Menu](/kit/overlay/menu).
</Lede>

<ArticleMeta />

## What it is

The headless [ContextMenu](/headless/menu/context-menu) with a themed panel and rows. The trigger
element's `contextmenu` event is intercepted: the browser menu is suppressed and the panel opens at
the pointer position, scroll included. Focus moves to the first enabled item. ArrowDown and ArrowUp
move between enabled items and wrap, Home and End jump to the ends, Enter or Space activates, and
Escape or a click outside closes the menu and restores focus to where it was.

The panel and rows are themed exactly like Menu: a `surface.raised` card at least 180px wide, rows
that take `surface.interactive` on hover and keyboard focus, and `text.muted` for a disabled item.

## Anatomy

  </ContextMenu.Trigger>
  <ContextMenu.Content>
    <ContextMenu.Item>Item</ContextMenu.Item>
    <ContextMenu.Separator />
    <ContextMenu.Item>Item</ContextMenu.Item>
  </ContextMenu.Content>
</ContextMenu.Root>`} />

## Usage

<CodeBlock lang="tsx" code={`import { ContextMenu } from '@usemotif/ui';

<ContextMenu.Root>
  <ContextMenu.Trigger>
    <FileRow file={file} />
  </ContextMenu.Trigger>
  <ContextMenu.Content>
    <ContextMenu.Item onSelect={cut}>Cut</ContextMenu.Item>
    <ContextMenu.Item onSelect={copy}>Copy</ContextMenu.Item>
    <ContextMenu.Separator />
    <ContextMenu.Item disabled>Paste</ContextMenu.Item>
  </ContextMenu.Content>
</ContextMenu.Root>`} />
