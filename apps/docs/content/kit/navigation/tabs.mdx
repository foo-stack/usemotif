# Tabs

<Lede>
  Tabs show one panel of related content at a time, picked from a row of labels. Selection follows
  focus: arrowing to a tab activates it.
</Lede>

<ArticleMeta />

## What it is

The headless [Tabs](/headless/disclosure/tabs) with a themed list, tabs, and panels. The headless
layer sets `role="tablist"`, `role="tab"` with `aria-selected`, and `role="tabpanel"`, and links
each tab to its panel. ArrowRight and ArrowLeft (ArrowDown and ArrowUp when `orientation` is
`vertical`) move to the next or previous enabled tab and wrap; Home and End jump to the first and
last.

The kit list is a row with a `border.default` bottom border. Each tab is a button in `text.muted`
with a transparent underline; the selected tab turns `action.primary` with a matching underline,
driven by its own `aria-selected` through the `_selected` style state. A panel is a `section` with
top padding.

## Anatomy

<CodeBlock lang="tsx" code={`<Tabs.Root defaultValue="a">
  <Tabs.List>
    <Tabs.Tab value="a">A</Tabs.Tab>
    <Tabs.Tab value="b">B</Tabs.Tab>
  </Tabs.List>
  <Tabs.Panel value="a">Panel A</Tabs.Panel>
  <Tabs.Panel value="b">Panel B</Tabs.Panel>
</Tabs.Root>`} />

## Usage

<CodeBlock lang="tsx" code={`import { Tabs } from '@usemotif/ui';

<Tabs.Root defaultValue="account">
  <Tabs.List>
    <Tabs.Tab value="account">Account</Tabs.Tab>
    <Tabs.Tab value="billing">Billing</Tabs.Tab>
    <Tabs.Tab value="api" disabled>API</Tabs.Tab>
  </Tabs.List>
  <Tabs.Panel value="account">
    <AccountSettings />
  </Tabs.Panel>
  <Tabs.Panel value="billing">
    <BillingSettings />
  </Tabs.Panel>
</Tabs.Root>`} />

Controlled:

<CodeBlock lang="tsx" code={`const [tab, setTab] = useState('account');

<Tabs.Root value={tab} onValueChange={setTab}>
  {/* list and panels */}
</Tabs.Root>`} />
