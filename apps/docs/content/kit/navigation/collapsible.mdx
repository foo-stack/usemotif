# Collapsible

<Lede>
  Collapsible hides one section behind a header until someone asks for it. It suits content most
  people skip: advanced settings, a long explanation, a secondary list.
</Lede>

<ArticleMeta />

## What it is

The headless [Collapsible](/headless/disclosure/collapsible) with a themed container. The trigger
is a button that toggles the panel and carries `aria-expanded` and `aria-controls`; the panel is a
`role="region"` labelled by the trigger. It is the single-section shape that
[Accordion](/kit/navigation/accordion) is built from.

The kit draws one bordered, rounded container. The trigger is a full-width button with a chevron
on the right; when open, its text turns `action.primary` and the chevron flips, driven by its own
`aria-expanded` through the `_expanded` style state. The panel sits under a hairline, in the `sm`
size and `text.muted`.

## Usage

<CodeBlock lang="tsx" code={`import { Collapsible } from '@usemotif/ui';

<Collapsible.Root defaultOpen>
  <Collapsible.Trigger>Advanced options</Collapsible.Trigger>
  <Collapsible.Content>
    <AdvancedSettings />
  </Collapsible.Content>
</Collapsible.Root>`} />

Controlled:

<CodeBlock lang="tsx" code={`const [open, setOpen] = useState(false);

<Collapsible.Root open={open} onOpenChange={setOpen}>
  {/* trigger and content */}
</Collapsible.Root>`} />
