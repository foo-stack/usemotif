# Accordion

<Lede>
  Accordion is a stack of Collapsibles linked through context. A `type` of `single` or `multiple`
  decides whether opening one panel closes the others.
</Lede>

<ComponentDemoStrip demo="accordion" />

<ArticleMeta />

## What it is

A compound component. `Accordion.Root` owns the set of open item ids and the open policy.
`Accordion.Item` wraps each section - it is a [Collapsible](/headless/disclosure/collapsible)
keyed to a stable `value`. `Accordion.Trigger` and `Accordion.Content` are Collapsible's Trigger
and Content, re-exported.

The `type` prop is the whole behavioural choice. `single` keeps at most one panel open - opening
a second closes the first. `multiple` lets any number stay open at once.

### Accordion.Trigger / Accordion.Content

Collapsible's `Trigger` and `Content`, re-exported. See the
[Collapsible API](/headless/disclosure/collapsible#api) for their props.

## Accessibility

Each item is a disclosure region - its trigger carries `aria-expanded` and `aria-controls`, its
content is a `role="region"` labelled by the trigger. A screen-reader user hears, for each
header, whether the panel is open and can step through them.

`type="single"` is closer to the WAI-ARIA accordion pattern; `type="multiple"` is a stack of
independent disclosures. With `collapsible={false}` the open item's trigger carries
`aria-disabled="true"`, as the pattern asks for a panel that cannot be collapsed. Pick `single` when the sections are alternatives - an FAQ where one
answer at a time is enough - and `multiple` when a user may want to compare panels side by side.

## Examples

A single-open FAQ:

<CodeBlock lang="tsx" code={`<Accordion.Root type="single" defaultValue={['q1']}>
  <Accordion.Item value="q1">
    <Accordion.Trigger><Button variant="ghost" fullWidth>How do refunds work?</Button></Accordion.Trigger>
    <Accordion.Content><Text>Refunds are issued within five days.</Text></Accordion.Content>
  </Accordion.Item>
  <Accordion.Item value="q2">
    <Accordion.Trigger><Button variant="ghost" fullWidth>Can I change my plan?</Button></Accordion.Trigger>
    <Accordion.Content><Text>Switch plans any time from settings.</Text></Accordion.Content>
  </Accordion.Item>
</Accordion.Root>`} />

A single-open group that always shows one panel:

<CodeBlock lang="tsx" code={`<Accordion.Root collapsible={false} defaultValue={['shipping']}>
  <Accordion.Item value="shipping">...</Accordion.Item>
  <Accordion.Item value="returns">...</Accordion.Item>
</Accordion.Root>`} />

A multi-open settings group:

<CodeBlock lang="tsx" code={`<Accordion.Root type="multiple" defaultValue={['profile', 'notifications']}>
  <Accordion.Item value="profile">...</Accordion.Item>
  <Accordion.Item value="notifications">...</Accordion.Item>
</Accordion.Root>`} />
