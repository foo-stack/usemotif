New `Toggle`, a button that stays pressed (`aria-pressed`, `data-state`), and `ToggleGroup` with `ToggleGroupItem`, a row of toggles holding one (`type="single"`) or several (`type="multiple"`) pressed values. The group keeps one tab stop, moves focus with the arrow keys along its `orientation` (wrapping, skipping disabled items) and Home / End, and can be disabled as a whole. Both work on React Native as toggle buttons.
