New `Listbox`, an always-visible list of options. It is a focusable `role="listbox"` whose `aria-activedescendant` follows the arrow keys and Home / End over enabled options, jumps by type-ahead, and selects with Enter, Space or a click; `selectionMode="multiple"` toggles several. It shares its option rows with `Combobox.List` and works on React Native.
