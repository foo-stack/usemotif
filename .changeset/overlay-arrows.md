An `Arrow` part for Popover, Tooltip and HoverCard: `Popover.Arrow`, `Tooltip.Arrow` and `HoverCard.Arrow`. It sits on the edge facing the trigger and points at the trigger's centre, moving to the other edge when the surface flips and staying on the trigger when it shifts. `computePosition` and `useFloatingPosition` report the arrow position as `arrow`. Headless arrows take `size`, `borderColor`, `borderWidth` and `style`. The kit's arrows match each surface. On React Native the part renders nothing. `FloatingArrowProps` is exported.
